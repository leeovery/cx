SPEC CONTEXT:
Spec §925 (specification.md:925) is the load-bearing constraint behind item (1): `prefs.json` needs **two decodes that must differ** — the tolerant load path (missing/empty/unrecognised → per-field default, no error) and the strict write-path re-read that errors on malformed JSON, because a tolerant decode in a writer turns a stray comma into a zero record that is then committed, erasing `session_list_mode`, `theme_migrated`, every untouched theme key and the preserved raw `appearance` in one keypress. The refactor therefore may share the *read* but must not share the *decode*. Items (2) and (3) have no direct spec clause — they are internal-consistency remediations from the duplication analysis; the panel floor's own contract (`themePanelFloor` is THE render-floor predicate; entry and resize must consume its answer rather than derive their own) is the property item (2) protects.
