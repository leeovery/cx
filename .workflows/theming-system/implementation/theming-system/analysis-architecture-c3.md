- FINDING: `ResolveSetting` takes three positional strings where the concrete keys type already exists
  SEVERITY: medium
  FILES: internal/theme/setting.go:114, internal/theme/setting.go:184, cmd/open.go:804, internal/tui/theme_panel.go:660, cmd/doctor_theme.go:202
  DESCRIPTION: `ResolveSetting(theme, light, dark string)` is the single collapse point for the whole theme setting, and every caller holds a struct with exactly those three fields (`theme.RawKeys` or `prefs.ThemeKeys`) and destructures it into three same-typed positional arguments at the call site — `theme.ResolveSetting(keys.Theme, keys.Light, keys.Dark)` in cmd/open.go, `m.themeState.keys.Theme/.Light/.Dark` in the panel, and again inside `InForceKeys`. Three adjacent `string` parameters where light and dark are interchangeable at the type level is exactly the inversion hazard the package elsewhere went to real lengths to close: `AdaptivePair`/`MemberPalette`/`Member.Palette` exist precisely so a palette cannot reach the pair untagged, with a comment warning that "reading it the wrong way round at one call site inverts light and dark for the whole product — with every palette still loading, every token still resolving and nothing failing anywhere". The same failure is reachable one layer down through this signature, and nothing in the type system objects. The signature also forces the control-strip to be re-run on values that are already stripped at three of four call sites (documented as safe because idempotent) and forces `RawKeys` to be rebuilt field-by-field at the prefs boundary (cmd/doctor_theme.go:202, and structurally in cmd/open.go).
  RECOMMENDATION: Take the value, not the parts: `ResolveSetting(keys RawKeys) Setting`, with stripping moved into a single `RawKeys` constructor/normaliser (e.g. `theme.NewRawKeys(theme, light, dark)` or `RawKeys.Stripped()`) that the two prefs-boundary sites in `cmd` call once. `InForceKeys` then passes its `RawKeys` straight through instead of destructuring, and no caller can transpose the pair.

  FILES: cmd/doctor.go:95-99, cmd/doctor_theme.go:141-152, cmd/doctor_theme.go:165-173, cmd/doctor_theme.go:281-291, cmd/doctor_theme.go:367-396
  DESCRIPTION: `advisory` is doctor's second, general report-line class, but two of its three fields (`slug`, `fromPrefs`) exist solely to serve the theme producers' one-slug-one-line union, and the renderer reads neither. - FINDING: `themeState.nomination` is refreshed after every commit with no consumer
  SEVERITY: low
  FILES: internal/tui/theme_panel_commit.go:342-349, internal/tui/theme_state.go:20-28, internal/tui/model.go:1386-1411
  DESCRIPTION: `applyCommittedSetting` maintains `themeState.nomination` post-commit, and the field carries a stated contract ("it describes the setting CURRENTLY IN FORCE, not only the one injected at construction — every commit that lands re-resolves it, so a reader past the gate may trust it"). But the field has exactly three readers, all of them construction- or gate-transition-only: `newNominationGate` (in `New`), `hasNomination` and `Nomination.Select` (both inside `syncResolvedMode`, which is reachable only from `New`, `armAppearanceDetection`, and the two gate arms guarded by the once-only `resolve()`). Keeping an unread field with an asserted invariant is the shape that quietly acquires a wrong consumer.
